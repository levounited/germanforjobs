FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you choose your next step.

How does it work after purchase?

Complete your payment securely through Stripe. The digital learning file is delivered separately; payment does not automatically email a download. If you need help with access, contact info@germanforjobs.com with your purchase details.

Can I also book a personal lesson?

Yes. Personal 60-minute lessons can be arranged by contacting us. Lesson packages and availability are confirmed before booking.

Do I need a subscription?

No. The interactive learning materials are offered as one-time purchases, with no recurring subscription required.

Which level is right for me?

Start with the free level quiz or try a free A1, A2, B1 or B2 exercise. The quiz is a learning guide, not an official language assessment.

Can I use the exercises on my phone or tablet?

Our interactive exercises are designed primarily for laptops and desktop computers. Some exercises may also work on smartphones and tablets, depending on your device and browser. For the best learning experience, we recommend using a computer.

What if I have a question about my order?

Email info@germanforjobs.com or contact us through WhatsApp Business. Include your order information so we can help.
